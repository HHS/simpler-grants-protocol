## OpportunityBase

A funding opportunity, such as a grant or loan. It shares its identity fields with [OppRef](#oppref), including the [OppIds](#oppids) collection under `identifiers` added in v0.5. Since v0.5 it also records who is offering the money under `funders`, an [OrgRefCollection](/protocol/models/organization#orgrefcollection), which replaces the deprecated [`agency` custom field](/custom-fields/agency/) for opportunities.

## OpportunityDetails

A funding opportunity with additional details, like available competitions. Since v0.5 it also lists the awards the opportunity produced under `awards`, a list of [AwdRef](/protocol/models/award#awdref) entries, so a consumer can see what an opportunity funded without searching awards by `opportunityId`.

An entry is a reference, not the award record: `GET /awards/<id>` is the canonical record only where an implementation serves the award routes, and otherwise the entry's `identifiers`, where the publisher supplies them, are the key for joining the award in another system. A consumer must not assume a reference can be dereferenced through this API. An absent `awards` means the implementation does not publish this link for the record, while an empty array means it does and no awards are known. Entries carry no ordering guarantee.

## OppRef

A reference to an opportunity, previewing the key fields a consumer needs to identify it and look it up or join it to another response. Appears wherever another record points to an opportunity, such as the opportunity an [award](/protocol/models/award) resulted from. Since v0.5 it also carries [OppIds](#oppids) under `identifiers`, so a consumer can match the opportunity on a published registry code rather than the hosting system's own id.

## OppIds

A collection of identifiers associated with an opportunity, keyed by registry code. It extends the generic [IdentifierCollection](/protocol/fields/identifier#identifiercollection) with the base identifiers CommonGrants defines for opportunities. Additional registries can be published under `otherIds`.
