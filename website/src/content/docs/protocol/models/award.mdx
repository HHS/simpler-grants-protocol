## AwardBase

A grant award, tracking the funder, recipient, and financial details of a grant that resulted from an opportunity. It references the source [opportunity](/protocol/models/opportunity#oppref) and [application](/protocol/models/application#appref), and its funders and recipient organizations via [OrgRef](/protocol/models/organization#orgref). Since v0.5 that opportunity link is also published in the other direction: an opportunity's detail record can list the awards it produced under [`OpportunityDetails.awards`](/protocol/models/opportunity#opportunitydetails). Since v0.5 the routes that serve this model are optional rather than experimental, so an implementation may omit them, but a breaking change to them now requires incrementing the `Major` protocol version; see [Award routes](/protocol/specification#award-routes).

## AwdRef

A reference to an award, previewing the key fields a consumer needs to identify it and look it up or join it to another response. Appears wherever another record points to an award, such as a parent award for an amendment or tranche, or, since v0.5, the awards an opportunity produced on [OpportunityDetails](/protocol/models/opportunity#opportunitydetails).
